# Sound credits

The texture loops in `public/samples/` are made from these recordings. Each was
trimmed to a short stretch, crossfaded into a seamless loop, level-matched,
peak-limited and encoded as AAC by `scripts/prepare-textures.mjs`.

| Texture | Recording | Author | Licence |
| ------- | --------- | ------ | ------- |
| Waves (`waves.m4a`) | [Waves and Sea](https://freesound.org/s/730461/) | FreqWincy | [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/) |
| Rain (`rain.m4a`) | [Spectrally Matched Rain Ambient - Part 5](https://freesound.org/s/855740/) | ekilyekil | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |

The waves recording is licensed for **non-commercial use only**. Replace it
before using Drift in anything commercial.

The felt-piano notes are synthesized by `scripts/render-samples.mjs`.
